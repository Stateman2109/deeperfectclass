Mastering the Quadratic Formula

Great choice! Quadratic equations take the form ax² + bx + c = 0. The Quadratic Formula is your "universal tool" for finding the roots (x-values) when factoring is too difficult.

x = -b ± √(b² - 4ac)2a

Key Insights:

  • Discriminant (b² - 4ac): Tells you how many solutions exist.
  • If positive: 2 real solutions.
  • If zero: 1 real solution.
  • If negative: No real solutions (complex).
